Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 15, 2026

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Passport Photo Maker is designed with privacy first. Your photos never leave your device, and we don't collect any personal information. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Photos and Images

What we collect: None. All photo processing happens entirely on your device (client-side).

  • Your photos are NEVER uploaded to our servers
  • Your photos are NEVER stored on our servers
  • Your photos are NEVER shared with third parties
  • Everything stays on your device

1.2 Local Storage Data

We use your browser's local storage (NOT cookies) to enhance your experience. This data stays on your device and includes:

  • Theme Preference: Light/dark mode selection
  • Language Preference: Your chosen language (English, Spanish, etc.)
  • Photo History: Thumbnails of your recent edits (stored locally for quick access)
  • Custom Presets: Your saved editing presets
  • Onboarding Status: Whether you've seen the tutorial

1.3 Analytics & Tracking

We do NOT use:

  • ❌ Google Analytics
  • ❌ Facebook Pixel
  • ❌ Any tracking cookies
  • ❌ Any third-party analytics
  • ❌ Any advertising networks

2. How We Use Your Information

Since we don't collect personal information, there's nothing to use! The local storage data mentioned above is used solely to:

  • Remember your preferences (theme, language)
  • Provide quick access to your recent edits
  • Save your custom presets
  • Improve your user experience

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT share, sell, rent, or trade your information with anyone.

The only external services we use are:

  • GitHub Pages: For hosting the website (they don't collect personal data from our app)
  • Google Fonts: For typography (no tracking, privacy-first CDN)

4. Your Rights & Control

4.1 GDPR (European Users)

If you're in the EU, you have these rights:

  • Right to Access: You can view all data stored locally in your browser (check browser DevTools → Application → Local Storage)
  • Right to Deletion: Clear your browser's local storage to delete all data
  • Right to Portability: Export your presets and history (download feature available in app)
  • Right to Object: Simply don't use local storage features

4.2 CCPA (California Users)

California residents have these rights:

  • Right to Know: We've disclosed all data collection above
  • Right to Delete: Clear browser data anytime
  • Right to Opt-Out: Not applicable (we don't sell data)
  • No Discrimination: App works fully without any data storage

4.3 How to Delete Your Data

To clear all locally stored data:

  1. Open your browser settings
  2. Go to Privacy/Site Data
  3. Find this website's data
  4. Click "Clear Data" or "Remove"

Alternatively, use your browser's "Clear Browsing Data" feature.

5. Data Security

Since all processing happens on your device, your data is as secure as your device. We recommend:

  • Keep your browser and operating system updated
  • Use a secure internet connection
  • Don't use public/shared computers for sensitive photos
  • Clear browser data after using shared devices

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you're a parent and believe your child has used our service, please contact us.

7. International Users

This website is hosted in the United States. However, since all processing happens on your device and we don't collect data, there are no international data transfers.
